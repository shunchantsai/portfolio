# Portfolio: Systems Geography, Climate Risk & Geopolitical Analysis

I am an analyst working at the intersection of physical geography, climate and catastrophe risk, and geopolitical systems. My work is grounded in physical and social science methodology — BSc Geography, MSc Climate Change, and MRes Urban Sustainability and Resilience, all at University College London — and is applied to systemic risk across environmental, infrastructural, and political domains.

## Approach

I separate structural drivers from surface noise — tracing how physical processes, institutional features, material incentives, and information environments produce systemic outcomes that are difficult to observe in real time and therefore systematically underpriced in conventional risk assessments. The work spans climate-linked infrastructure vulnerability, catastrophe-risk modelling practice, and geopolitical systems, with particular attention to Taiwan’s strategic environment and high-mountain hazard corridors. I use iterative AI-assisted workflows for research synthesis while maintaining full editorial control over analytical outputs.
